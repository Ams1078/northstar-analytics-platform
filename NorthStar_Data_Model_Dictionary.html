Global conventions

These rules hold across every table, so they are documented once here rather than repeated in each entry.

Canonical sourceEvery fact carries a DataSource column. DataSource = 1 is the canonical truth. Any query not filtered to it blends pipelines and is not meaningful.
Date keysAll date keys use the YYYYMMDD integer form.
Surrogate keysAll keys are integers. Business codes are never used as keys.
Technical keysKey columns are hidden from the Power BI field list; only descriptive attributes and measures are exposed.
NullabilityKeys and metrics are non-null. Forward-looking snapshot columns may be null before their window opens.
Namingfact_ for facts, dim_ for dimensions and lookups, YYYYMMDD for date keys, and a Key suffix for surrogate keys.
Stored vs derivedColumn tables list physical stored columns only. Cross-fact calculations such as CPL or Occupancy % are DAX measures, documented in the Measure Catalog.
