Semantic Model

The NorthStar semantic model organizes marketing, leasing, and operational data into a dimensional star schema optimized for Power BI. It provides a single analytical layer where every measure resolves through conformed dimensions, allowing consistent reporting from the property level to the portfolio.

01  Model Overview

Property and date are the two conformed dimensions of the model, and property is its analytical hub. Every fact carries PropertyKey and relates directly to property; geographic filtering reaches the facts only through it, flowing region to market to property. Date connects to every fact through its own separate relationship. The process-specific marketing dimensions connect selectively on the right. Dimensions filter facts; facts never filter dimensions.

02  Design Principles

The model follows standard enterprise star-schema practice, enforced across every table and relationship.

Star schemaFacts at the center, dimensions on the edges. No flattening into wide tables.
Conformed dimensionsShared dimensions filter every fact consistently, so a region or property means the same thing everywhere.
Single-direction filteringDimensions filter facts, never the reverse. Filter direction is always explicit.
One fact, one processEach fact table models a single business process at a single grain.
Shared calendarA single active date dimension governs all daily facts.
Controlled geographic snowflakeRegion and market are kept as separate lookup tables for governed geographic filtering, while facts connect at property grain. This is a deliberate hybrid, not a pure flat star.
No bidirectional filtersBidirectional relationships are never used.
Dimension-driven slicingAll filtering originates from dimensions, not from the facts.

Six conformed dimensions filter the facts. Each carries a surrogate integer key and the attributes used for slicing and grouping.

Channel path. dim_channel and dim_funnel_stage are small lookup tables supporting channel and funnel-stage reporting. The prospect journey carries its own ChannelKey, the channel recorded on the individual touch, which is the active path for channel analysis. The vendor also has an assigned channel, carried as a descriptive attribute on dim_vendor, not as a second active relationship into the same fact. Keeping one active channel path per fact avoids an ambiguous relationship path.

06  Date Intelligence

The model carries two date tables, which is deliberate. A single calendar cannot serve both daily reporting and lease-dated events without one distorting the other. Separating them keeps both clean, and lets the lease calendar extend further into the future than the fact data without leaving gaps in daily reporting.

07  Model Decisions

The structural choices below were made deliberately and hold across the model.

Why separate fact tables?
Marketing, leasing, and operations run at different grains, refresh on different cadences, and answer to different business owners. Splitting them keeps each fact honest to its own process rather than forcing a single wide table.
Why conformed dimensions?
Shared dimensions give consistent filtering across every fact, reduce model size, and guarantee that a region or property means the same thing everywhere.
Why a vendor dimension?
It supports attribution, marketing rollups, and channel reporting from one place, and keeps vendor attributes out of the facts.
Why keep separate region and market tables?
Property already carries market and region as columns, but region and market are also kept as separate lookup tables so geographic entities can be governed and filtered on their own. This is a deliberate hybrid: a controlled geographic snowflake layered on an otherwise flat star, chosen because reporting needs region-, market-, and property-level logic.
Why two date tables?
A single calendar cannot serve both daily reporting and lease-dated events without one distorting the other. Separating them keeps both clean.

08  Naming Standards

Object names follow a fixed convention, so the role of any object is readable from its name. This maps directly to the conventions documented in the Measure Catalog.

Prefix or suffixMeaning

09  Performance

The model is built for query performance and maintainability, following the practices below.

  • Star schema over flattened tables
  • Integer surrogate keys on every relationship
  • Single-direction filters throughout
  • Shared dimensions to reduce model size
  • Technical columns hidden from the field list
  • Measures over calculated columns
  • Variable-driven DAX to avoid recomputation
  • Display measures separated from base measures
